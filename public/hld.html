1. Executive Overview & Problem Context

In urban Ethiopia, finding a residential or commercial rental property has historically been monopolized by informal middlemen (delalas). EthioServe Rent provides a direct, trustworthy alternative by enforcing verified landlord credentials, direct tenant-owner messaging, transparent ETB pricing, and integrated mobile money checkout.

3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture

The platform enforces an Authentication Gate. Every user must authenticate to access the features tailored to their role:

Role Core Capabilities & Access Scope Restricted Capabilities
Tenant (House Seeker) • Explore & search properties across Ethiopian cities
• Filter by Sub-City, Type, and ETB budget
• Save favorite homes
• Direct chat with Landlords & direct phone call (+251)
• Submit star ratings and landlord reviews
• Report fraudulent listings or broker fee demands 		Cannot post properties, manage landlord portfolios, or access the admin control center.
Landlord (Property Owner) • Access Landlord Hub dashboard
• Post new properties via 4-step wizard
• Manage vacancies (toggle Available vs. Occupied)
• Boost listings to Premium (300 ETB) or Gold (800 ETB) via Telebirr/CBE
• Submit Kebele / National ID for the Verified Landlord Shield
• Reply to tenant inquiries directly 		Cannot access the system administrative moderation and audit center.
Administrator (Trust & Safety) • Access Admin Dashboard & real-time KPIs
• Audit & approve/reject Landlord ID submissions
• Triage tenant fraud reports & remove scam listings
• Suspend/reactivate rogue user accounts
• Monitor platform revenue generated from Telebirr/CBE boosts 		Full system governance and moderation rights.

4. Core Functional Modules

4.1 Authentication & Profile Module

Supports login via Ethiopian phone number or email with password, role-specific registration (Tenant vs Landlord), and instant 1-click evaluation personas for testing (Almaz, Dawit, Solomon, and Abebe).

4.2 Property Catalog & Regional Search

Supports granular location search: Addis Ababa (Bole, Yeka, Kirkos, Arada, Nifas Silk, etc.), Hawassa, Bahir Dar, Adama, and Dire Dawa. Features verified infrastructure attributes (3-phase backup generator, water reserve tank, 24/7 security).

4.3 Landlord Identity Verification Subsystem

Landlords upload scans of their Kebele ID, National ID (Fayda), or Passport. The admin audits the document and grants the green Verified Landlord Shield.

4.4 Anti-Delala & Fraud Prevention Engine

Includes duplicate photo hash detection, price anomaly warnings (<35% of sub-city median), and direct incident reporting with instant de-listing capabilities.

4.5 Mobile Money & Monetization Gateway

Simulates USSD push payments with Telebirr, CBE Birr, and Chapa for 300 ETB (Premium) and 800 ETB (Gold Boost) listing promotions.
